# Primary Storyline Revelation Map 
Kai, a young poor man, savaged by his lack of ability, resources and relationship seeks power at all costs so that he may never feel the inadequecy of powerlessness ever again. But is this what he really wants? Will the single minded pursuit of power lead to a bittersweet isolation from above?

1. Young Pup - Growth. Him and his people aggressively savaged during the Initiation Royale. Embarressed and ignored at a party thrown by students to celebrate the new year. He vows to never be savaged again. Arc encompasses raw stat training. 
2. Loyal Dog - Relationships. Works with others. But still weak. Arc introduced other named characters working to be better and working together. 
3. Underdog - Hierarchy. Forced to be a follower during parties and never recognized as a leader which is required to move up in tiers. Arc potentially prioritizes his own growth versus the growth of others so he may be a leader. Arc focuses on the fact that you have so many 
4. Rabid Dag - Betrays others. 
5. Proud Wolf - Betrays himself (and his morals). Kai will do anything to be the #1 contender. Final confrontation with The Dragon. 
6. Man - Reconciliation with all parts of yourself. On the onset of girls versus guys royale he finds out the school had been fueling his journey. This throws into flux his entire worldview that he'd been a dog struggling and fighting his way to the top all by himself despite everyone else working against him. How can he reconcile the grit and determination he thought were the keys to his victory with the knowledge that this had been preordained?
